# Support policy

## Categories and priority

| Category | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| safety-hazard | P1 |
| damaged-item | P2 |
| late-delivery | P3 |
| other | P3 |

## Refunds

- A damaged item reported within 30 days of delivery is refunded in full for the item.
- Shipping is never refunded.
- After 30 days, offer store credit instead of a refund.
- Never promise a replacement; say a replacement is checked separately.
