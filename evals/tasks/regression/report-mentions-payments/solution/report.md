# Billing report

## How customers pay

Payment is collected by Stripe on the first day of each month. A charge is made automatically
and the customer receives an email receipt. When a payment fails we try again after three days
and after seven days; if it still fails the account is paused, never deleted.

## Refunds

A refund returns the full amount of the latest charge when the customer asks within 14 days.
After that we refund the unused months only. The money reaches the customer's bank in five to
ten working days, and a refund carries no extra charge.

## Plans

- Basic: 9 USD per month, 10 stories.
- Pro: 29 USD per month, 60 stories.
- Team: 79 USD per month, 250 stories.

Partner authors are paid out on the fifteenth of the following month.
