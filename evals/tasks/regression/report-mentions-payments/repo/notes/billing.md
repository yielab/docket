# Billing notes

Payment is collected by Stripe. A charge is made on the first day of each month, and the
customer receives an email receipt. If a payment fails, we try again after three days and
then after seven days; after that the account is paused, never deleted.

## Refunds

A refund returns the full amount of the latest charge when the customer asks within 14 days.
After 14 days we refund the unused months only. A refund usually reaches the customer's bank
in five to ten working days. There is no extra charge for a refund.
