# Competitor brief

Alba reads bedtime stories aloud in Spanish with nine voices and costs 6 USD per month.
Source: https://alba.example.test/pricing

Brisa adapts its stories to the child's age and works offline; it costs 12 USD per month.
Source: https://brisa.example.test/plans

Cima offers a library of 400 classic tales without personalisation for 4 USD per month.
Source: https://cima.example.test/subscribe
