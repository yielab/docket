# Competitor notes

Gathered by the research team last week; every fact carries its source.

## Alba

- Bedtime stories read aloud in Spanish, nine voices.
- Price: 6 USD per month.
- Source: https://alba.example.test/pricing

## Brisa

- Stories adapted to the child's age, offline mode.
- Price: 12 USD per month.
- Source: https://brisa.example.test/plans

## Cima

- A library of 400 classic tales, no personalisation.
- Price: 4 USD per month.
- Source: https://cima.example.test/subscribe
